<size=120%><b>CHAPTER V | TELEPHONE</b></size>

<b>THE FINAL LINK IN THE CHAIN</b>

Every completed dispatch must be confirmed by telephone.

The rotary telephone connects the operator to the Ministry's authorized receiving departments.

The correct telephone number is determined exclusively by the telegram's DESTINATION field.

<size=110%><b><color=#8B2929>5.1 OFFICIAL TELEPHONE DIRECTORY</color></b></size>

Civilian Postal Office: 101
Government Bureau: 204
Military Command: 315
Industrial Administration: 426
Intelligence Directorate: 537
International Dispatch: 648

<size=110%><b><color=#8B2929>5.2 DIALING PROCEDURE</color></b></size>

1. Verify the destination indicated by the deciphered telegram.
2. Locate the corresponding department in the official directory.
3. Dial all three digits using the rotary telephone.
4. Confirm the completed dispatch.

Do not confuse the identification code with the destination. The identification code is used only for deciphering; the destination determines the telephone number.

The Ministry will evaluate the submitted dispatch instructions and telephone destination.

<b>THE VOICE OF THE MINISTRY MUST NEVER TREMBLE.</b>
