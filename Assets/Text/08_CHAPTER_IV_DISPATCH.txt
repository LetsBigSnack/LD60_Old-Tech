<size=120%><b>CHAPTER IV - DISPATCH</b></size>
Once the telegram has been deciphered, its instructions must be transferred to a dispatch punch card. A standard telegram contains four fields:
<b>DESTINATION</b>: Receiving department
<b>TYPE</b>: Classification of correspondence
<b>HANDLING</b>: Required processing procedure
<b>REFERENCE</b>: Administrative reference designation

<size=110%><b><color=#73654a>4.1 DISPATCH CARD CONFIGURATION</color></b></size>
Column 1: Destination

Column 4: Reference

For standard dispatch operations, punch exactly one row per column.
