<size=120%><b>CHAPTER II | MORSE CODE</b></size>

<b>THE LANGUAGE OF THE TELEGRAPH</b>

All incoming communications arrive in International Morse Code.

DOT: . (short signal)
DASH: - (long signal)

Each letter is separated by a space. Words are separated by /. Line breaks indicate separate lines of the telegram.

<size=110%><b><color=#8B2929>2.1 INTERNATIONAL MORSE ALPHABET</color></b></size>

B -...     O ---

E .        R .-.
