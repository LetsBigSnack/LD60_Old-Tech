<size=110%><b><color=#8B2929>1.2 STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE</color></b></size>

1. Receive the incoming Morse transmission.
2. Identify the first three letters using the Morse reference chart.
3. Prepare a deciphering punch card using the identification code and current date.
4. Insert the card into the deciphering apparatus.
5. Read the decrypted telegram.
6. Prepare a dispatch punch card according to the telegram's instructions.
7. Insert the card into the dispatch terminal.
8. Dial the appropriate departmental telephone number.
9. Confirm the completed transmission.

<b>DO NOT IMPROVISE. DO NOT ASSUME. FOLLOW PROCEDURE.</b>

<size=110%><b><color=#8B2929>1.3 PERFORMANCE AND REMUNERATION</color></b></size>

Correctly completed transmission: +20
Incorrectly completed transmission: -10
Unfinished transmission: 0

A transmission is evaluated only upon final confirmation. No additional compensation is awarded for speed.

Each working day is strictly limited. Any unfinished transmissions remaining at the end of your shift will be transferred to the night shift for processing, without penalty to your performance record.

<b>THE MINISTRY REWARDS PRECISION, NOT EXCUSES.</b>
