<size=110%><b><color=#8B2929>4.6 COMPLETE DISPATCH EXAMPLE</color></b></size>

<b>DECIPHERED TELEGRAM:</b>

<b>DESTINATION: MILITARY</b>
<b>TYPE: CLASSIFIED</b>
<b>HANDLING: FORWARD</b>
<b>REFERENCE: B</b>

Consult the four tables.

Column 1: MILITARY = Row 3
Column 2: CLASSIFIED = Row 3
Column 3: FORWARD = Row 5
Column 4: B = Row 2

<b>REQUIRED PUNCH PATTERN: 3 - 3 - 5 - 2</b>

Insert the prepared card into the dispatch terminal.

A dispatch card is an official administrative order. Errors may result in delays, misclassification, or unauthorized destruction of correspondence.

<b>A SINGLE HOLE MAY CHANGE THE DESTINY OF A THOUSAND LETTERS.</b>
