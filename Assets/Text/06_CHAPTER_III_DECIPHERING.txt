<size=120%><b>CHAPTER III - DECIPHERING</b></size>
To protect the nation's correspondence, the Ministry employs mechanical encryption.

Each transmission requires a deciphering punch card prepared from its identification code and the current day of the month.

[PAGE]
<size=110%><b><color=#73654a>3.1 THE PUNCH CARD</color></b></size>
Every standard Ministry punch card contains 4 vertical columns, 6 numbered rows, and 24 possible punching positions.

For standard deciphering operations, punch exactly one hole in each column.
Column 1: First identification letter
Column 2: Second identification letter
Column 3: Third identification letter
Column 4: Current day of the month
[PAGE]
Additional holes may alter the deciphering result. Incorrectly punched cards should be discarded and replaced.
