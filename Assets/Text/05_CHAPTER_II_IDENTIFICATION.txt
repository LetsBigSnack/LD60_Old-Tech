<size=110%><b><color=#73654a>2.4 IDENTIFYING THE TRANSMISSION</color></b></size>
Every incoming telegram begins with a three-letter identification code.

These first three letters are transmitted without encryption.

The remaining message is encrypted and must be processed by the deciphering apparatus.
[PAGE]
<b>EXAMPLE</b>
-.- --- --
Decode each letter individually:
<b>-.- = K</b>
<b>--- = O</b>
<b>-- = M</b>
<b>IDENTIFICATION CODE: KOM</b>

Record these letters carefully. They determine the required deciphering punch card.
