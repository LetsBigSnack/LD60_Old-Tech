# Comrade!

It's time to decrypt secret morse messages, punch some holes and forward the messages to the proper department. So get your keypuncher ready and try to get that sweet promotion! What how? Tsk...

Stick to your instruction manual, you guess you digress, is what they say, right? And don't forget you have the whole working week prove yourself! Proof your worth, comrade.


## About the Game

COMMIE-nication is a "Papers, please" inspired game in which you take the role of a Communications Officer in the fictitious nation of East-West Slavia. Your job is to make sure that all the Message and Letters arrive save and sound and more importantly on time. Our People value their privacy and we are proud to say that our encryption system is one of the best worldwide. This is good for the customers but bad for you, you have to work extra hard to get all necessary information you need to correctly process the transmissions. Your typical days looks as follows decypher morse code, send disptach instruction via punch card, call the right department for them to accept the transmission and repeat. At the end of each Day you will get paid based on your performance and at the end of each working week your performance will be evaluated strictly.
